#   2. TIMELINE SCAN. What these accounts post and retweet is read directly.

# TUNING
# ------
# - 20 to 80 handles is the practical range. Below ~15 the MIN_SMART_FOLLOWS

#   sweep gets slow and the signal dilutes toward "crypto twitter in general".

#   generate noise, not signal, and they drown out everyone else.
# - Re-seed after each mint you win or miss: add whoever was early, drop whoever
#   never fires. A frozen list decays.
